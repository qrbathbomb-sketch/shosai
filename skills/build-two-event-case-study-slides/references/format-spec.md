# Two-event case-study format specification

## Source of truth

Use `../assets/two-event-case-study-reference.pptx`. The measurements below document its repeated structure; the source slide itself takes precedence.

## Canvas and visual system

- Slide size: 13.333 × 7.5 in, 16:9
- Background: white
- Primary typeface: Yu Gothic (`游ゴシック`)
- Weight: bold for every visible text element, including titles, labels, body copy, English kickers, and page numbers
- Primary text: `#0B0B0B`
- Secondary text: `#6B6B6B`
- Hairline divider: `#E7E7E7`
- Apparel accent: coral `#FF6D5A`
- Cosmetics accent: magenta `#E94677`
- Do not add shadows, rounded cards, borders around images, gradients, patterns, or decorative icons.

## Typography

- Set every visible text element to Yu Gothic bold. Preserve each existing point size and text-box geometry from the reference deck.
- Event title: 20 pt bold. Shorten only unofficial descriptors, never the official name.
- Field labels: 12 pt bold.
- Overview and date values: normally 12–13 pt.
- Venue value: normally 11–12 pt when needed for a long official venue name.
- Page number: 10 pt.
- Use full-width Japanese punctuation consistently. Avoid trailing periods in short headings.
- Never squeeze text by horizontal scaling. Rewrite or wrap first.

## Cover and divider slides

- Main cover: white background, thick black top rule, large black Japanese title at upper-left, small English kicker, two short accent rules, ample white space.
- Category divider: full-bleed event photograph with large white two-line title and a short accent rule. Maintain a dark enough crop or overlay only when needed for legibility.
- Keep divider copy minimal: category name plus `POP-UP 実績` and `SELECTED WORKS`.

## Image selection and cropping

- Prefer a wide establishing shot that immediately communicates the event environment.
- Use organizer or brand press imagery when available.
- Avoid logos-only images, posters containing most of the slide's information, low-resolution social screenshots, and collages.
- Keep products, people, signage, and spatial features recognizable after the 6.23 × 3.44 in crop.
- Do not crop off the main activation or stretch the image to fit.
- Never place the top, bottom, or side edge of an image frame through a person's face or head. Preserve comfortable space around visible faces; if the required crop cannot do this, use another verified photograph.

## Copy examples

Good overview:

> 新作リップの先行販売とデコレーション体験を、フルーツパーラーを模した空間で展開。

Avoid:

> ブランドの魅力を存分に体感できる、次世代型の特別なポップアップイベントを実施。

The first sentence names observable components. The second is promotional, vague, and unsupported.

## Odd counts and missing evidence

- Do not duplicate an event to fill a page.
- Do not fabricate a venue, date, role, or result.
- If one image is missing, use a clearly labeled placeholder only in a working draft. Do not present it as final.
- If a final slide has one case, retain the left-column geometry and purposeful white space on the right unless the user provides a different approved layout.
